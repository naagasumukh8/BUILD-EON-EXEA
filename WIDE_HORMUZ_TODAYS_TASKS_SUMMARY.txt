3. DYNAMIC NATURAL LANGUAGE INTAKE & DEMAND PARSING
================================================================================
- Built a smart natural language intake parser in Python (backend/routers/intake.py) 
  and TypeScript (lib/api.ts) capable of understanding unstructured operational prompts 
  such as:
  * "I need 2 million barrels of diesel delivered to India in 7 days."
  * "0.2 billion barrels of crude to Japan within 30 days."
  * "500,000 bbl gasoline to Rotterdam within 14 days."
- Parsing logic automatically extracts:
  * Commodity type (Diesel, Crude Oil, Gasoline, LNG).
  * Volume required (handles billion/b, million/m, thousand/k, raw numbers, comma-separated formatting).
  * Delivery deadline (in days).
  * Destination port/country (Mumbai/India, Tokyo/Japan, Rotterdam/Europe, Singapore, Houston/USA, etc.).
  * Priority weights (cost vs time vs risk).

6. COMMERCIAL DEAL EVALUATOR & DETERMINISTIC P&L ENGINE
================================================================================
- Implemented a 100% deterministic arithmetic financial evaluator (backend/services/deal_evaluator.py & lib/api.ts).
- Computes landed cost per barrel, total outlay, expected profit, profit margin %, 
  target price ceiling, and commercial verdicts (GO / NEGOTIATE / REJECT).
- Applied strict Data Provenance Labeling:
  * AIS Live Stream: Positions & speeds only (never claims spare cargo capacity).
  * CANDIDATE_UNVERIFIED: Vessel found in AIS without confirmed charter party terms.
  * REAL_REFERENCE: Curated infrastructure data (Yanbu IPSA pipeline capacity & tariffs).
  * CALCULATED: Outputs derived mathematically by arithmetic engines.
  * CONFIRMED: Verified commercial charter quotes.

7. MULTI-MODAL STRATEGY OPTIMIZER (DYNAMIC ALLOCATIONS & 5 ALTERNATIVES)
================================================================================
- Completely removed fixed 30/40/30 allocation presets.
- Powered by OR-Tools continuous linear solver (with greedy fallback) in lib/optimizer.py 
  and lib/api.ts.
